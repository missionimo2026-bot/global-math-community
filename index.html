PROBLEM OF THE DAY

Can you solve it?

Find the smallest positive integer that leaves remainder 2 when divided by 3 and remainder 3 when divided by 5.

Answer: 8
8 ÷ 3 leaves remainder 2, and 8 ÷ 5 leaves remainder 3.

05 — ABOUT

Why Mission IMO?

Many students are curious about mathematical Olympiads but don't know where to start.

Mission IMO aims to create a place where students can discover mathematics, practise problem solving and learn from one another.

Our goal

Make high-quality mathematical learning accessible to young students everywhere.

11 — ABOUT

Why Mission IMO?

Many students are interested in mathematical Olympiads but don't know where to begin.

Mission IMO aims to create a place where young students can discover mathematics, practise problem solving and learn together.

Our mission

Make high-quality mathematical learning accessible to young students everywhere.
